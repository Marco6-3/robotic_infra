# 第一层允许使用的主张

> Under the preregistered finite-resolution observation model and controlled grasping environment, matched current tactile-proprioceptive-action observations can correspond to different contact outcomes within 100 ms. A predictor using causal interaction history improves prediction and matched-pair discrimination on unseen physics seeds, including a fixed-friction subset. This experiment does not establish an independent benefit of action conditioning, and the advantage does not hold for simulator-exact observations.

中文：在预注册的有限分辨率观测模型和该受控抓持环境中，当前触觉、本体与动作观测相近，
仍可能对应不同的 100 ms 内接触结局。使用因果交互历史的模型在未见物理种子上改善了预测和配对判别，
固定摩擦子集也有增益。但没有建立动作条件化的独立收益，该优势在 simulator-exact 观测下不成立。

禁止将此改写为历史具有普遍必要性、ContactBelief 已证明、真实传感器有效、多任务 SOTA 或真实世界泛化。
